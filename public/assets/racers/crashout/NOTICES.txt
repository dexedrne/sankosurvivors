Sanko artwork and assets
Kart models, equipment, textures, thumbnails and assembly data retain their existing project ownership and permissions. This package is an asset integration delivery for the agreed Sanko ecosystem project, not a public-domain or general open-source artwork release.

Third-party libraries
The original Three.js MIT licence is retained at public/crashout/assets/LICENSE-MIT.txt. The included Draco decoder licence is retained in full at public/crashout/assets/LICENSE-DRACO.txt. Any engine libraries installed separately retain their respective licences.

The optional integration helper performs asset resolution and scene assembly only. Racing, AI, wallet/authentication, currency and server implementation are not included.
