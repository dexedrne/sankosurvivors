Description:
The town is overrun. Pick your adventurer, find a build, and survive the hordes in the SankoQuest world.

Collect experience, choose weapons and passive upgrades, discover evolutions and take on bosses. Survive a map, then carry your build into Endless. Unlock ordinary companions, finish quests and achievements, and grow your adventurer between runs.

• Native SankoQuest models, world and music
• Touch controls, Tumble and a visible Pause button
• Casual, Hard and Endless runs
• Local progress with progress-code backup and transfer
• Bundled game content for offline play

This iPhone edition is a separate casual game with no paid entries, purchases or cryptocurrency features. Progress is stored on this device.

Beta review notes:
No account or login is required. Tap Enter the field to start. Pause is at the top right; use Pause → Quit run to view results. Settings has separate music/effects controls and graphics options. The first music playback may require a tap, as required by WebKit. There are no wallet prompts or paid modes. A first local save is created on this installation; existing website/PWA progress can be imported only through the user's progress code.
